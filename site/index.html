Quick Todo

A todo list for Android that opens instantly and forgets what you don't need.

Quick Todo is built for the things you need to do today and tomorrow. Tap the icon and your list is already on screen, ready for the next item. Anything you haven't renewed is cleared away after 30 hours, so the list never piles up with stale tasks.

What it does

  • Add as fast as you can type Type and press Enter. The box stays focused so you can add several items in a row.
  • Items expire on their own Every item has a 30-hour timer you can renew with a tap. Checked items are cleared at 1:00 am.
  • Calendar and history Plan tasks for future days, see what you did or missed each day, and look back at expired items.
  • Repeating tasks Schedule recurring tasks, such as every weekday morning, and they appear on the list when they're due.
  • Your data, your device No account, no ads, no tracking. Export everything to CSV whenever you like, and import it again later.

Quick Todo collects nothing. Your list is stored only on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
