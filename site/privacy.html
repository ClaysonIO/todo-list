Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

In short: Quick Todo collects nothing. Your todos stay on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to export your data and send it somewhere yourself.

What we collect

Nothing. Quick Todo has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The app does not send your todos, settings or any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Where your data is stored

Your todos, scheduled tasks, history and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We have no server and no copy of your data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

When data leaves your device

Only in these cases, all of which you control:

  • Export. When you tap export in Settings, the app creates a CSV file of your data and opens the Android share sheet. Where the file goes is up to you, for example email, a messaging app or cloud storage. Once it's there, that service's privacy policy applies.
  • Android backup. If you have turned on backup in your device settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. Google handles that backup. We cannot see or access it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. Android grants it internet access by default, but the app does not use it to send any data.

Children

Quick Todo collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before releasing that change.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/ClaysonIO/todo-list/issues.
